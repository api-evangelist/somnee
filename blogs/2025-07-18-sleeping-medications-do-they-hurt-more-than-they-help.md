According to Gallup's most recent state of sleep report, ⅓ of American adults describe their sleep as ranging from "poor" to "fair." That's about 84 million adults across the U.S. who struggle to consistently get deep, restorative sleep.1 And with the massive impact sleep has on everything from daily performance to our relationships to long-term health — it makes sense why many of us opt for sleeping pills as a "surefire" way to hit our sleep goals. The problem: A lot of sleeping drugs aren't as "surefire" as you might think...
