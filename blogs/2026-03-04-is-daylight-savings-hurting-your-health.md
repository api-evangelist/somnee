“Most people think nothing of losing an hour of sleep for a single night,” says Somnee Co-Founder Dr. Matt Walker. The truth: “It is anything but.” For ~⅓ of countries worldwide, including the 48 states that ‘Spring Forward’ an hour every March, the transition to daylight saving time (DST) is often seen as a temporary annoyance that ultimately brings a welcome boost to daylight hours, later sunsets, and longer evenings.
