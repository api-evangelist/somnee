Dad’s sleep often gets swept under the rug. But with ~30% of men experiencing insomnia and new fathers sleeping less than female partners — dad's sleep deserves a spotlight. Headlines have made the cost of chronically poor sleep clear: it’s bad for heart health, blood pressure, diabetes, memory, focus, and even longevity.
